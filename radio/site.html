Translation — live decoded reading

Captions appear once the station's readout has calibrated and you've tuned in.

Every buzz above is real: the amplitude comes straight from flybrain.FlyBrain — the actual 166,700-neuron MaleCNS connectome, stepped live — specifically each cast member's own wing-motor-neuron spikes above rest (the "song" flytalk.py already measures). Nothing is pre-rendered or looped. The ~190 Hz sine tone that envelope rides on, and the show names/branding, are a sonification layered on top — made up, plainly, same as this project's other demos. Captions are a genuine live decode (a cross-validated readout trained once at startup on the real contexts), not an echo of whichever context is driving a column, so they can legitimately read low-confidence or as the "wrong" context.
