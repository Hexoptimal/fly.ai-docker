THE WORK

A real brain, not a benchmark

Jobs drive one eye or touch channel and count what the fly's motor neurons do. Together they map which senses move the fly, at which settings.

CHECKED

Every answer can be checked

The brain runs in exact integer maths, so any GPU or CPU gives the same spikes. Some jobs are re-run on the server, and a wrong answer zeroes your day.

PAID MONTHLY

Points, then a pool

Link a wallet to keep your points. After each month the pool is split by points and you claim your share on Robinhood Chain.
