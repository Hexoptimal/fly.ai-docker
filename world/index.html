What this ishide

A field of fruit flies, each one flying on its own spiking neural network — 612 leaky integrate-and-fire neurons named after real Drosophila cell types and wired in the shape of the pathways measured in the MaleCNS connectome.

Nothing in a fly's behaviour is scripted. Light, odour puffs, airflow and leg contact are injected into sensory neurons; the network runs; and flight is read back out of the motor neurons. There is no if (nearFruit) goToFruit. Feeding, landing, escaping the swatter, plume tracking, courtship and egg-laying all fall out of the wiring — or fail to, which is reported just as plainly.

A full life runs here: flies eat, mate, lay eggs, hatch, hunt for food, get eaten by spiders and die — each of those drops a marker on the map where it happened. The clock runs a 300-second day, and night is real to the flies: it dims the luminance their photoreceptors see, rather than setting any “it is night” flag.

Click any fly to watch its brain fire. Every bar, tag and number in this panel is a readout of population firing rates, never a driver of them.

It is not the real connectome: 612 neurons against 166,700, with real cell-type names but invented counts and weights. Nothing here is trained. What is and isn't real →

Wiz · a fly working a monkeyasleep

A giant monkey wizard puppeted by the full fruit fly connectome — 166,700 neurons and 25 M synapses, running in your browser. The strings are real neurons: what he sees goes into the fly's looming, threat and target cells, his footsteps into its leg-touch cells, and the descending neurons that fire raise his arms, kick his feet into missteps, turn his head, steer him and make him hop. Only his wish to wander is coded; his balance, steps and falls are simulated (springs and IK) — nothing in a fly brain knows how to walk a monkey. The fly overhead is a cartoon of that brain: its strings light up green when their neurons fire. The flies see him loom. Downloads about 58 MB.
