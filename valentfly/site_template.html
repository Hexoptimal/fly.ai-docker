Control room — live descending-neuron reading, per date

Every date, every reading, and the final proposal above is the real output of flybrain.FlyBrain — the actual 166,700-neuron MaleCNS connectome, simulated spike by spike. There is no model and nothing is trained: each candidate has one dedicated real neuron population; dating them means stimulating that population for 0.5 s at a strength set by their true compatibility, then reading the settled mean spike trace of the brain's own descending neurons. The proposal always goes to whichever candidate produced the strongest live reading. The "compatibility by scent" assignment of seats to neuron populations is made up for this demo — nothing here claims a real fly picks mates this way.
